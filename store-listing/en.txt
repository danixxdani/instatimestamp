Instagram only shows “2h”, “3d” or “1w” — never the exact time a post was published. InstaTimestamp fixes that automatically.

Open Instagram and the full date and time, down to the second, appears right next to every timestamp, in the timezone you choose. No hovering, no clicking, no digging through page code.
